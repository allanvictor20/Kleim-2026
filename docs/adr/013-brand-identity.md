# ADR-013: Kleim brand identity: blue and pink theme from the logo

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** [date]
- **Related:** UI/UX Style Guide §2 and §4; SDD §20

## Context

The first prototypes used a placeholder warm-neutral theme with a wine accent and a serif display font. The team has since chosen the name Kleim and a logo: a K built from a deep-to-sky blue gradient with a hot-pink ribbon.

## Decision

Adopt a theme taken directly from the logo. Kleim Blue 700 (#0043D5) is the primary action colour; Blue 600 (#0060F3) marks links and selection; Pink 500 (#FC4B98) is a small accent for hearts, badges and urgency, with Pink 700 (#B8176A) for any pink text. The blue gradient is reserved for one hero moment per screen. Plus Jakarta Sans replaces Fraunces and DM Sans as the single type family. Light and dark themes are both supported.

## Consequences

- Positive: one coherent identity across app, documents and marketing; one font family keeps pages lighter.
- Negative: blue is common among delivery and payment apps, so the logo and pink accent must carry recognition; pink needs care for contrast.
- Follow-up: commission flat two-colour and one-colour versions of the logo before any printing (bags, stickers, receipts).

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not chosen |
| --- | --- |
| Keep the warm wine theme | Clashes with the logo colours |
| Pink as the primary action colour | White text on the logo pink fails contrast; pink everywhere loses its impact |
